Materials

A controlled material system
built for functional performance

Materials are organized into three tiers — General, Functional, and Reinforced — covering the majority of functional use cases. Standardized production profiles are applied to every order.

Material Tiers

General / Functional / Reinforced

I Need To See It

General

For parts where the primary goal is evaluation.

If you need to hold it, inspect it, test fitment, assemble it, or understand how the design works in the real world, General is usually the right place to start.

I Need To Use It

Functional

For parts intended for real-world use.

Parts produced to perform, not just to represent the finished product.

Examples

  • Brackets
  • Mounts
  • Enclosures
  • Fixtures
  • Utility parts

I Need To Depend On It

Reinforced

For demanding environments and higher-performance applications.

Reinforced materials are intended for applications where increased stiffness, thermal performance, or other application-specific requirements make fiber-reinforced materials appropriate.

Examples

  • Load-bearing applications
  • Outdoor environments
  • Elevated-temperature environments
  • Demanding service conditions

Every Reinforced project is reviewed prior to manufacturing to confirm the material is appropriate for the application.

Color

Black, by default

Black is the standard production color across all material tiers.

This provides a clean, uniform appearance and supports consistency across parts and production runs. If your project has specific color requirements, include that detail when you submit your request and we will let you know what is available.
