How We Work

The same process,
every order

Every order at SFN_MFG follows the same process. Before production begins, we review the file — confirming printability, evaluating geometry, assessing orientation, and verifying material selection. If anything needs to be addressed, we reach out before we print. Final pricing and timeline are confirmed before work starts.

During production, we apply standardized profiles, controlled material selection, and dimensional and visual inspection to every job. Documentation is included with every production order.

The Technology

Additive manufacturing as a production process

Additive manufacturing builds parts layer by layer from a digital model. Unlike traditional subtractive methods — where material is cut away from a block — it adds only what is needed. The result is a process that scales efficiently for low-volume production.

Additive manufacturing remains cost-effective for prototypes, replacement parts, and low-volume production where traditional tooling would be impractical. No dies, no molds, no setup fees.

SFN_MFG operates a production-focused setup built around a controlled material system covering three performance tiers: General, Functional, and Reinforced. Not sure which material is right for your project? See Materials or get in touch.
