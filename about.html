Our Story

San Diego roots,
global reach

We started Tugboat Manufacturing because we kept running into the same problem: getting a physical part made was either too slow, too expensive, or required minimum orders that made no sense for a one-person operation or a small team. San Diego has a thriving maker and engineering community, and we wanted to build something that served it — and everyone else who needs real parts without the runaround.

3D printing has come a long way. Today's machines produce parts that are dimensionally accurate, mechanically functional, and visually clean enough for end-use applications. Whether you're an engineer testing a new bracket, a small business owner who needs a custom enclosure, or a hobbyist who wants something you simply can't buy off the shelf — we can make it. Upload your STL or STEP file, choose your material, and get a quote instantly. No phone calls, no back-and-forth, no minimum orders.

We're a small operation, and we like it that way. Every order gets our full attention. If something's off about your file or your tolerances look tight, we'll reach out before we print.

The Technology

What makes 3D printing
so useful

Additive manufacturing works by building parts layer by layer from a digital model. Unlike traditional subtractive methods — where material is cut away from a block — 3D printing adds only what's needed, reducing waste and enabling geometries that would be impossible or prohibitively expensive to machine.

The result is a technology that scales beautifully for low-volume production. One part costs almost the same to make as ten. There are no dies to cut, no molds to pour, no setup fees. For rapid iteration, custom parts, and short runs, it's hard to beat.

We work primarily with FDM (fused deposition modeling) and resin-based processes, which cover the vast majority of use cases: structural parts, enclosures, brackets, display pieces, prototypes, and more. Not sure which process is right for your project? Get in touch and we'll help you figure it out.
