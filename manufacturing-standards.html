Manufacturing Standards

The same standards,
every project

Every project receives the same review process and manufacturing standards regardless of order size.

Before Manufacturing Begins

Every model is reviewed
before production starts

Every model is reviewed before manufacturing begins. The review covers:

  • Geometry
  • Printability
  • Orientation
  • Material suitability
  • Manufacturability

If anything requires clarification, SFN_MFG will contact the customer before production starts.

Production begins only after the project has been reviewed. Final pricing and timeline are confirmed before any work is started.

This process applies to every order — from a single prototype to a short production run.

During Manufacturing

Controlled process,
consistent output

Controlled Material Selection

Materials are selected and verified before production begins. All materials are sourced from 3D-Fuel and applied to a standardized, production-focused configuration.

■

Standardized Manufacturing Profiles

Consistent print parameters applied to every job. No ad-hoc settings — every profile is production-oriented.

■

Process Monitoring

Active monitoring during production to catch issues early and maintain consistent output across the run.

Dimensional & Visual Inspection

Completed parts are inspected for dimensional accuracy and visual quality before shipping.
