Manufacturing Standards

The same standards,
every project

Every project receives the same review process and manufacturing standards regardless of order size.

Before Manufacturing Begins

Every model is reviewed
before production starts

Every model is reviewed before manufacturing begins. The review covers:

  • Geometry
  • Printability
  • Orientation
  • Material suitability
  • Manufacturability

If anything requires clarification, SFN_MFG will contact the customer before production starts.

Production begins only after the project has been reviewed. Final pricing and timeline are confirmed before any work is started.

This process applies to every order — from a single prototype to a short production run.

Parts Policy

Parts are delivered
as-printed

Parts are delivered as-printed, with support material removed and support contact points smoothed and cleaned up as part of our standard quality handoff. We do not offer additional post-processing — including full-surface sanding, vapor or chemical smoothing, painting, coating, or multi-part assembly.

Layer lines and minor surface texture are an inherent characteristic of this manufacturing process, not a defect. If your application requires a specific surface finish or cosmetic result, let us know during the quote conversation so we can confirm whether we're the right fit before production begins.
