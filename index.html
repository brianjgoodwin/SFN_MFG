What We Do

Customer-supplied models.
Professionally manufactured parts.

SFN_MFG is a fabrication studio based in San Diego, California. We produce professionally printed prototypes, functional components, replacement parts, and small-batch production runs.

Every order receives a pre-production review. We confirm printability, evaluate geometry and orientation, and verify material selection before anything is printed. If something in your file needs to be addressed, we reach out before we print.

About SFN_MFG

A manufacturing process
designed for consistency.

Additive manufacturing is most effective when approached as a production process. Controlled materials, standardized production methods, documented inspection, and clear communication help ensure consistent results from quote through delivery.

Digital models become physical parts through a repeatable production process designed for prototypes, replacement parts, and low-volume runs. No dies, no molds, no setup fees.

SFN_MFG operates a production-focused setup built around a controlled material system covering three performance tiers: General, Functional, and Reinforced.
