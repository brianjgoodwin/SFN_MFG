Materials & Production

What we work with
and how we work

A focused material system built for functional performance and consistent output. All materials are U.S.-manufactured and sourced from 3D-Fuel.

Materials

Standard / Functional / Reinforced

Standard

For prototyping, fitment checks, and general-purpose parts. Optimized for dimensional accuracy and clean, consistent results.

Ideal for

  • Concept models
  • Fit checks
  • Display parts

Functional

For parts that need to perform in real-world use. Improved durability and impact resistance for components that will be handled, tested, or used repeatedly.

Ideal for

  • Brackets and enclosures
  • Fixtures
  • Everyday functional components

Reinforced

Carbon fiber and glass fiber reinforced materials for applications requiring increased stiffness and structural performance. Reviewed per project to confirm suitability.

Ideal for

  • Load-bearing components
  • Structural parts
  • High-stress applications

Color

Black, by default

Black is the standard production color across all materials.

This provides a clean, uniform appearance and supports consistency across parts and production runs.

Production Setup

Built for consistent output

Reinforced Material Capability

Reinforced materials introduce additional wear during printing.

SFN_MFG printers are configured with wear-resistant components to maintain consistent output and dimensional accuracy when processing carbon fiber and glass fiber filaments.

Production-Focused Configuration

The production setup is optimized for functional output — not decorative or novelty work.

This means every machine setting, material choice, and process decision is oriented toward parts that need to perform.

Quality & Print Review

Every job reviewed before we print

■

Pre-Production Review

Every project is reviewed prior to approval to ensure printability, structural integrity, and overall part quality. This includes evaluation of geometry, orientation, and material selection.

■

Print Optimization

Parts may be adjusted for optimal print orientation and performance. Recommendations may also be provided to improve strength, durability, or overall print success when applicable.

■

Final Approval

Production begins only after the project has been reviewed and approved. Final pricing and timeline are confirmed before any work is started.
